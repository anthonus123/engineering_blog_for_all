About

Hello! My name is Anthony Maalouly, and welcome to my blog!

Short Bio :
I did my undergraduate degree in mechanical engineering and minored in mathematics.
I worked for three years in an aircraft simulation company until I decided to go back to graduate school.
I researched collision avoidance for quadrotors in urban environments, and that was a fun and rewarding experience.
Now, I am working as a Robotics Control Developer, and I am looking forward to tackle this new challenge.

Why even do this blog? 
Well, that’s a good question. I think I have interesting ideas to share with my peers, and if they can be helpful to one person, then it would make me happy. Also, I always enjoy vulgarising various concepts to my surroundings, so this blog will hopefully give me this opportunity. Lastly, when I wrote my master thesis, I realized how difficult writing was, and the only way to get better at something is through practice.

What is this blog about?
I will talk about various topics in engineering, science, and hobbies of mine. If I do this correctly, then after reading a post, you will have learned something new 🙂
